# Project 05 Redaction Checklist

Before publishing, visually confirm the following are not visible:

- Company names
- Participant names
- Participant initials
- Avatars or visible faces
- Meeting titles
- Meeting transcript content
- Meeting chat content
- Tenant names and domains
- Dates and times
- Policy identifiers if sensitive
- Meeting IDs or telemetry identifiers
- Private AI recap text
- Any other confidential meeting context

Final review rule: the screenshot should still show the Teams Premium feature and task context.
